# Table of Contents for IIT JAM Mathematics

## Chapter 1: Fundamentals of Real Analysis
### Section 1.1: Sequences of Real Numbers
An introduction to the convergence of sequences, exploring bounded and monotone sequences, and Cauchy sequences, with the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem.

### Section 1.2: Series of Real Numbers
Detailed discussion on absolute convergence and tests of convergence for series, including comparison test, ratio test, and root test.

### Section 1.3: Power Series
Explore power series with a focus on the radius and interval of convergence, and techniques for term-wise differentiation and integration.

### Section 1.4: Functions of One Real Variable
Examine limits and continuity, the intermediate value property, differentiation, Rolle’s Theorem, and mean value theorem.

### Section 1.5: Advanced Calculus Concepts
Cover the L'Hospital rule, Taylor’s theorem and series, maxima and minima, and provide an introduction to Riemann integration.

## Chapter 2: Integral Calculus
### Section 2.1: Definite Integrals
Understand definite integrals and their properties, with a thorough look into the fundamental theorem of calculus.

### Section 2.2: Techniques of Integration
Explore various methods for solving integrals, including substitution and integration by parts.

### Section 2.3: Applications of Integration
Discuss practical applications such as calculating areas under curves and the concept of the average value of a function.

### Section 2.4: Numerical Integration
Introduction to numerical integration techniques including trapezoidal and Simpson’s rules.

## Chapter 3: Multivariable Calculus
### Section 3.1: Functions of Multiple Variables
Introduction to limits and continuity in higher dimensions, with an emphasis on the concept of partial derivatives and total derivative.

### Section 3.2: Optimization
Explore maxima and minima for functions of two or three variables, including the method of Lagrange multipliers.

### Section 3.3: Double and Triple Integrals
Examine the use and calculation of double and triple integrals, with applications in determining surface areas and volumes.

### Section 3.4: Change of Variables
Discuss techniques for changing the order of integration and transforming variables to simplify evaluation of integrals.

## Chapter 4: Introduction to Differential Equations
### Section 4.1: First Order Differential Equations
Study various types of first order differential equations including Bernoulli’s equation and exact differential equations.

### Section 4.2: Linear Differential Equations
Explore first and second-order linear differential equations, incorporating constant coefficients and solving techniques.

### Section 4.3: Special Methods of Solution
Learn integrating factors, method of separation of variables, and special solutions like orthogonal trajectories.

### Section 4.4: Advanced Solution Techniques
Delve into the method of variation of parameters and Cauchy-Euler equations for solving complex differential equations.

## Chapter 5: Linear Algebra - Basics
### Section 5.1: Systems of Linear Equations
Examine methods for solving systems of linear equations, focusing on the concepts of rank and nullity.

### Section 5.2: Properties of Matrices
Discuss matrix properties, including determinant and inverse, and explore the use of eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

### Section 5.3: Finite Dimensional Vector Spaces
Understand the structure of vector spaces, including linear independence, basis, and dimension.

### Section 5.4: Linear Transformations and Representations
Introduce linear transformations and their matrix representations, with discussions on range space and null space.

## Chapter 6: Abstract Algebra - Group Theory
### Section 6.1: Introduction to Groups
Explore the fundamental concepts of groups, including cyclic and abelian groups, and their properties.

### Section 6.2: Permutation and Non-Abelian Groups
Examine permutation groups and delve into the structure of non-abelian groups through examples.

### Section 6.3: Group Homomorphisms
Understand group homomorphisms and isomorphisms, with applications in quotient groups and direct products.

### Section 6.4: Advanced Group Theory Concepts
Study normal subgroups, Lagrange’s theorem for finite groups, and delve into creative problem-solving in abstract algebra.

## Chapter 7: Advanced Topics in Linear Algebra
### Section 7.1: Rank-Nullity Theorem
Provide an in-depth analysis of the rank-nullity theorem, its implications, and applications in solving linear systems.

### Section 7.2: Eigenvalue Problems
Discuss applications of eigenvalues and eigenvectors, featuring characteristic polynomials and diagonalization.

### Section 7.3: Matrix Decompositions
Cover various matrix decompositions, including LU, QR, and singular value decomposition (SVD).

### Section 7.4: Applications of Linear Algebra
Explore practical applications, such as computer graphics, cryptography, and network analysis.

## Chapter 8: Advanced Analysis Concepts
### Section 8.1: Uniform Convergence
Delve into uniform convergence and its implications on interchange of limits, integration, and differentiation.

### Section 8.2: Improper Integrals
Study the convergence and evaluation of improper integrals, including comparison tests and the Cauchy Principal Value.

### Section 8.3: Laplace Transforms
Introduce Laplace transforms as a tool for solving differential equations and transform methods in complex analysis.

### Section 8.4: Fourier Series and Transforms
Examine Fourier series and transforms, exploring how they apply in solving differential equations and signal processing.
```
This comprehensive table of contents provides a clear progression from fundamental concepts to advanced topics in mathematics, catering to the requirements of the IIT JAM Mathematics exam.
