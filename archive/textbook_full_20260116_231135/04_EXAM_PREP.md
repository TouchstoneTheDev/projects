## 1. EXAM STRUCTURE & FORMAT

### Overview of Exam
- **Exam Title**: IIT Joint Admission Test for M.Sc. (JAM) - Mathematics
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Mode**: Computer-based test
- **Total Questions**: Around 60 questions
- **Sections**: 
  - Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  - Section B: Multiple Select Questions (MSQs)
  - Section C: Numerical Answer Type (NAT)

### Time Allocation
- **Section A**: 30 questions (50 marks)
- **Section B**: 10 questions (20 marks)
- **Section C**: 20 questions (30 marks)
- Recommended Time:
  - Section A: 90 minutes
  - Section B: 30 minutes
  - Section C: 60 minutes

### Scoring Rubric
- **Section A (MCQs)**:
  - +1 or +2 marks per question
  - Negative marking: -1/3 or -2/3 marks respectively
- **Section B (MSQs)**:
  - +2 marks per question
  - No negative marking
- **Section C (NAT)**:
  - +1 or +2 marks per question
  - No negative marking

## 2. STUDY PLAN (6 Weeks)

### Week 1-2: Real Analysis & Functions
- **Topics**: 
  - Sequences and series
  - Functions of one real variable
- **Focus**: Convergence tests, Taylor series, Integrals
- **Practice**: Solve sequence problems, simulate real analysis questions from previous papers
- **Time Management**: 3 hours a day, including breaks

### Week 3: Multivariable Calculus & Differential Equations
- **Topics**: 
  - Multivariable functions, double/triple integrals
  - Differential equations
- **Focus**: Calculating areas and volumes, solving differential equations
- **Practice**: Work on higher order differential equations
- **Time Management**: 2-3 hours of focused study, incorporate visual learning tools

### Week 4: Linear Algebra & Basic Algebra
- **Topics**: 
  - Matrix theory, systems of equations
  - Permutations, combinations
- **Focus**: Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, binomial theorem
- **Practice**: Solve linear transformation problems, attempt combinatorial problems
- **Time Management**: 3 hours split into theory and problem-solving

### Week 5: Abstract Algebra & Group Theory
- **Topics**: 
  - Cyclic, abelian groups
  - Group homomorphisms
- **Focus**: Understanding group structures, permutations
- **Practice**: Small group discussions on group properties
- **Time Management**: 2-3 hours, break concepts into smaller parts

### Week 6: Revision and Mock Practice
- **Activities**: 
  - Revise all topics
  - Take mock exams under timed conditions
- **Focus**: Identify weak areas, practice time management on full mock tests
- **Time Management**: Simulate exam day, take multiple short breaks

## 3. 100+ PRACTICE PROBLEMS

### Real Analysis & Sequences
1. **Basic**: Determine if the sequence (1/n) converges.
2. **Intermediate**: Prove the convergence/divergence of the series ∑ (1/n^2).
3. **Advanced**: Discuss the convergence of the power series ∑ n^nxn.

### Multivariable Calculus
1. **Basic**: Evaluate double integral of f(x,y) = x + y over the region R.
2. **Intermediate**: Find the maximum/minimum of f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 subject to x + y = 1.
3. **Advanced**: Triple integrate f(x,y,z) = xyz over a given cubic region.

### Differential Equations
1. **Basic**: Solve dy/dx = y(1-y) using separation of variables.
2. **Intermediate**: Find the particular solution of the differential equation y'' + y = cos(x).
3. **Advanced**: Use the method of variation of parameters to solve x^2y'' + xy' + y = 0.

### Linear Algebra
1. **Basic**: Determine if a given set of vectors is linearly independent.
2. **Intermediate**: Find the eigenvalues of matrix A = [[2,3],[3,2]].
3. **Advanced**: Verify the rank-nullity theorem for a given transformation.

### Group Theory
1. **Basic**: Show that any subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic.
2. **Intermediate**: Prove a group is abelian iff for all a, b in G, (ab)^2 = a^2b^2.
3. **Advanced**: Determine the order of the symmetric group S3.

**Solutions**: For all problems above, detailed step-by-step solutions will be available along with hints and common missteps discussed.

## 4. LAST-MINUTE TIPS

### Key Formulas to Memorize
- **Taylor Series**: f(x) = Σ (f^n(a)/n!)*(x-a)^n
- **Eigenvalues**: det(A - λI) = 0
- **Integral Properties**: ∬_R f(x,y) dx dy

### Common Traps
- Misjudging limits and continuous domains
- Skipping steps in transformations or substitutions

### Time Management Strategies
- Tackle easier questions first to secure time
- Allocate time for review & comprehension checks

### Day-Before Checklist
- Rest adequately, review only key formulas
- Prepare accessories: admit card, ID, stationery
- Plan logistics for the exam day - travel, meals
