## **Chapter 1: Real Analysis**

### **1.1 Sequences and Series of Real Numbers**

#### **Learning Objectives**
- Understand the principles of convergence and sequence types.
- Utilize various tests to determine convergence of series.
- Analyze power series and their properties.

#### **Key Concepts and Definitions**
- **Sequence**: An ordered list of numbers defined by a specific rule.
- **Convergence**: A sequence or series approaches a fixed value (limit).
- **Cauchy Sequence**: A sequence where the distance between successive terms approaches zero.
- **Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem**: Every bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence.
- **Absolute Convergence**: A series converges precisely when the series of absolute values converges.

#### **Detailed Explanation**
A sequence is a fundamental concept in real analysis, where we explore how the terms behave as they approach infinity. A sequence {a_n} converges to a limit L if for every ε > 0, there exists an N such that for all n ≥ N, |a_n - L| < ε. In simpler terms, as we progress through the sequence, the terms become arbitrarily close to L.

Bounded and monotone sequences offer insight into sequence behaviors. A sequence is bounded if there is a real number M such that |a_n| ≤ M for all n. Monotone sequences are either entirely non-increasing or non-decreasing, which often aids in analyzing convergence.

#### **Examples and Case Studies**

1. **Convergent Sequence Example**
   Consider the sequence a_n = 1/n. As n tends to infinity, a_n approaches 0, so we conclude that the sequence converges to 0.

2. **Cauchy Sequence Example**
   Analyze b_n = 1/n^2. We find for any ε > 0, there exists an N such that 1/n^2 < ε for all n ≥ N, indicating that b_n forms a Cauchy sequence.

3. **Power Series Application**
   Explore the power series Σx^n. Its radius of convergence is 1, allowing for term-wise differentiation and integration within the interval (-1, 1).

#### **Real-World Applications**
- **Financial Mathematics**: The concept of sequences is instrumental when evaluating annuities and compound interest.
- **Engineering Signal Processing**: Series are used to approximate continuous signals which are critical in digital communications.

#### **Practice Questions**

1. Determine whether the sequence a_n = (-1)^n/n converges.
2. Using the ratio test, find the convergence of the series Σ(n^2/2^n).
3. Evaluate the radius of convergence for the power series Σn!x^n.

#### **Summary Points**
- A sequence's convergence can be proved using limits.
- Series convergence is critically assessed with several tests, including comparison, ratio, and root tests.
- Power series display unique properties enabling differentiation and integration term-by-term.

### **Review Questions**

1. Prove the Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem with an example.
2. Discuss the implications of absolute convergence in the series Σ of your choice.

#### **Learning Outcomes**
After completing this section, students will be able to:
- Identify and analyze various types of sequences and series.
- Apply convergence tests effectively to assess series behavior.
- Recognize the power series and compute its radius and interval of convergence.
