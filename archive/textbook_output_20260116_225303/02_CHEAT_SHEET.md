#### **Section 1: Real Analysis**

**Core Formulas & Theorems:**

- **Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem**: Every bounded sequence in \(\mathbb{R}\) has a convergent subsequence.
- **Tests for Series Convergence**:
  - **Comparison Test**: \(\sum a_n\) converges if \(a_n \leq b_n\) and \(\sum b_n\) converges.
  - **Ratio Test**: \(\lim_{n \to \infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| < 1 \Rightarrow \sum a_n\) converges.
  - **Root Test**: \(\limsup_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|} < 1 \Rightarrow \sum a_n\) converges.

**Key Definitions:**

- **Cauchy Sequence**: A sequence \((a_n)\) in \(\mathbb{R}\) is Cauchy if \(\forall \epsilon > 0, \exists N \text{ such that } |a_n - a_m| < \epsilon \) for all \(n, m > N\).

**Quick Reference:**

- **Power Series**: \(\sum c_n(x-a)^n\) converges if \(|x-a| < R\), where \(R\) is the radius of convergence.

**Memory Aids:**

- **DRIP** for convergence tests: **D** for Direct Comparison, **R** for Ratio, **I** for Integral, **P** for p-Series.

**Common Mistakes:**

- Confusing absolute convergence with conditional convergence.

#### **Section 2: Multivariable Calculus and Differential Equations**

**Core Formulas & Theorems:**

- **Mean Value Theorem for Integrals**: If \(f\) is continuous on \([a, b]\), then \(\exists c \in (a, b)\) such that:
  \[
  \int_a^b f(x)\,dx = f(c)(b-a)
  \]

- **Chain Rule (Multivariable)**: \(\frac{\partial z}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}\)

**Common Techniques:**

- **Separation of Variables**: For \(y' = g(y)h(x)\), rewrite as \(\frac{dy}{g(y)} = h(x)dx\) and integrate both sides.

**Memory Aids:**

- **SOHCAHTOA** for trigonometric derivatives in polar coordinates: **S**in = **O**pposite/**H**ypotenuse, etc.

**Problem-Solving Flowchart:**

1. Identify if the problem involves single or multivariable calculus.
2. For differential equations, check type:
   - Linear, separable → Proceed with corresponding methods.
   - Verify initial/boundary conditions if provided.

**Quick Derivations:**

- **Partial Derivative**:
  \[
  \frac{\partial f(x, y)}{\partial x} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h, y) - f(x, y)}{h}
  \]

#### **Section 3: Linear Algebra and Algebra**

**Core Formulas & Theorems:**

- **Rank-Nullity Theorem**: For a linear transformation \(T: V \to W\), \(\dim(V) = \text{rank}(T) + \text{nullity}(T)\).

- **Eigenvalue Equation**: For matrix \(A\), \(Ax = \lambda x\) where \(\lambda\) are the eigenvalues and \(x\) are the eigenvectors.

**Key Definitions:**

- **Cyclic Group**: A group generated by a single element.

**Common Techniques:**

- **Row Reduction**: Utilize Gaussian elimination for finding rank and solving systems of equations.

**Memory Aids:**

- **COLD** for systems of equations: **C**onsistent, **O**verdetermined, **L**inear dependence, **D**ependence on variables.

**Notation & Conventions:**

- Matrices are denoted by uppercase letters, e.g., \(A, B\).
- Vectors and column matrices are written in bold, e.g., \(\mathbf{v}\).

**Common Mistakes:**

- Confusing linear independence with orthogonality.
- Overlooking the need for eigenvectors normalization.
