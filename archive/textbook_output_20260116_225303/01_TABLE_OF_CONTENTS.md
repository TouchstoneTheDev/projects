# Table of Contents

## Chapter 1: Fundamentals of Real Analysis

### 1.1 Sequences of Real Numbers
- Introduction to sequences, convergence, and properties of sequences such as boundedness and monotonicity.

### 1.2 Series of Real Numbers
- Comprehensive examination of series, including absolute convergence and various tests including comparison, ratio, and root tests.

### 1.3 Power Series
- Exploration of power series, their convergence radius, and the calculus of power series through term-wise differentiation and integration.

## Chapter 2: Functions of One Real Variable

### 2.1 Limits and Continuity
- Detailed analysis of limits and continuity, including the intermediate value property.

### 2.2 Differentiation
- Study of differentiation, supported by Rolle’s and the mean value theorems, along with the application of L'Hôpital's rule.

### 2.3 Taylor’s Theorem
- Understanding Taylor's theorem and series, focusing on their use in approximating functions.

### 2.4 Riemann Integration
- Principles of Riemann integration and the fundamental theorem of calculus, concentrating on definite integrals and related properties.

## Chapter 3: Multivariable Calculus

### 3.1 Functions of Multiple Variables
- Introduction to limits, continuity, and partial derivatives in functions of two or three variables.

### 3.2 Extrema in Multiple Variables
- Analysis of maxima and minima in multivariable functions, using critical points.

### 3.3 Multiple Integrals
- Detailed instruction on double and triple integrals, and application in calculating surface areas and volumes.

## Chapter 4: Differential Equations

### 4.1 First Order Differential Equations
- Study of Bernoulli's equation, exact differential equations, and integrating factors.

### 4.2 Orthogonal Trajectories and Homogeneous Equations
- Exploration of orthogonal trajectories and solutions of homogeneous first-order differential equations.

### 4.3 Linear Differential Equations
- Focus on second order linear differential equations with constant coefficients and methods like variation of parameters.

### 4.4 Cauchy-Euler Equations
- Resolution of Cauchy-Euler equations and their role in practical scenarios.

## Chapter 5: Principles of Linear Algebra

### 5.1 Systems of Linear Equations
- Analysis of systems of linear equations, rank, nullity, and the rank-nullity theorem.

### 5.2 Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
- Study of determinants, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors and their significance.

### 5.3 Linear Transformations
- Examination of finite-dimensional vector spaces, including concepts of linear independence, basis, and linear transformations.

## Chapter 6: Algebra and Groups

### 6.1 Basic Algebra and Combinatorics
- Fundamental principles of permutations, combinations, and the Binomial Theorem.

### 6.2 Group Theory Essentials
- Introduction to cyclic, abelian, and non-abelian groups, including permutation and normal subgroups.

### 6.3 Advanced Group Theory
- Detailed study of quotient groups, Lagrange’s theorem for finite groups, and group homomorphisms.

## Chapter 7: Advanced Topics in Analysis

### 7.1 Advanced Series and Integrals
- Exploration of series and integrals beyond the basics, including uniform convergence and improper integrals.

### 7.2 Metric Spaces
- Introduction to the topology of metric spaces, open and closed sets, and compactness.

### 7.3 Functional Analysis
- Preliminary concepts in functional analysis, focusing on Banach and Hilbert spaces.

## Chapter 8: Comprehensive Applications

### 8.1 Real-World Applications in Sciences
- Application of mathematical principles to solve real-world problems in physics and engineering.

### 8.2 Computational Techniques
- Numerical methods for solving calculus and linear algebra problems, emphasizing practical computation.

### 8.3 Advanced Research Topics
- Introduction to research-level topics and recent advancements in the mathematical sciences.

# Appendices

## Appendix A: Mathematical Tables
- Standard mathematical tables for quick reference.

## Appendix B: Answers to Selected Problems
- Solutions to selected problems for self-assessment and verification.

This comprehensive Table of Contents provides a structured pathway for understanding key mathematical concepts crucial for success in the IIT JAM Mathematics examination. Each chapter and section is thoughtfully designed to guide students from basic to advanced topics.
